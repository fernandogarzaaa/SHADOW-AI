# Shadow — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-24_

Shadow is a local-first personal AI agent. This policy explains what the Shadow
mobile app does and does not do with your data.

## Summary
- The app stores **no personal data on our servers** — we don't operate one.
- Your memory and activity live on **your Shadow Node** (a server you run), where
  they are stored **encrypted**.
- The app's only network connection is to the **node URL you configure**.

## Data the app handles
- **Connection setting:** the node URL you enter is stored locally on the device.
- **Your prompts, memory, and actions:** sent only to your configured node, never
  to us. The node treats retrieved memory as untrusted and gates cloud model use
  behind your explicit consent and approval.
- **Provider API keys (optional):** if you connect a frontier model provider, the
  key is sent to your node and stored there (encrypted). It is **not** stored in
  the app or transmitted to us.

## Data we do NOT collect
- No analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers, or third-party trackers.
- No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
- No account or sign-up with us.

## Third parties
If you choose to connect a frontier model provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google),
prompts you escalate to the cloud are processed by that provider under **their**
privacy policy. This only happens with your explicit per-request approval.

## Your control
- You can clear app data by deleting the connection setting or uninstalling.
- All memory deletion, consent revocation, and audit review happen on your node.

## Contact
For privacy questions, contact the operator of your Shadow deployment.
